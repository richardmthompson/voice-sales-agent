# ADR-016: Email Capture via Frontend Field, Not Voice

**Date:** 2026-05-26
**Status:** Accepted

## Context

V2 sends a structured summary email to prospects after conversation. This requires capturing their email address. Two approaches exist:

1. **Voice collection** — The bot asks for the email address, the STT transcribes it, and a tool validates and stores it.
2. **Frontend field** — A persistent text input on the UI where the user types their email.

Voice transcription of email addresses is highly error-prone. Spelling out "r-i-c-h-a-r-d at a-r-l-a-b-s dot t-e-c-h" defeats the purpose of a voice agent — it forces the user into a frustrating correction loop ("did you say r-i-c-h-a-r-d?" "yes" "at a-r-l-a-b-s dot t-e-c-k?" "no, t-e-c-h"). The conversation shifts from value-generating discovery to data entry.

## Decision

We will use a persistent email input field on the frontend UI. Email is submitted to the backend via `POST /email` with the session ID. The bot never collects email by voice. The system prompt acknowledges the email field's existence and may verbally nudge the user to "type your email in the field on screen" at appropriate moments.

If the conversation ends without an email, the closing screen highlights the email field with a tooltip ("Enter your email to receive a summary of this conversation!"). If the user enters their email post-conversation, the summary email is sent immediately.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Eliminates transcription errors for structured data (email addresses)
- Email capture is non-blocking — user can enter it at any point during or after the conversation
- Keeps the voice channel focused on high-value discovery conversation
- Post-conversation email capture is a second chance — user sees the summary preview and is motivated to enter their email

### Negative

- Requires frontend UI work (email field, POST endpoint, localStorage state management)
- Users on mobile may find typing during a voice call awkward (mitigated by allowing post-conversation entry)
- The bot cannot confirm email receipt in real-time speech (it gets notified via RTVI data channel, but the natural acknowledgment moment may have passed)

### Neutral

- The `POST /email` endpoint adds one new HTTP route to the voice server, alongside the existing `POST /start`
- localStorage tracks conversation state (`idle` / `in_progress` / `completed`) to manage email field visibility across page refreshes
